# How to Take a Special Exam - Student Guide

## Before You Start

### Preparation Checklist
- [ ] Check internet connection works (open Google.com)
- [ ] Use a reliable browser: Chrome, Firefox, or Safari (recommended)
- [ ] Clear browser cache if having issues
- [ ] Have a backup device ready (phone, tablet)
- [ ] Have water/snacks nearby (no eating while exam shows)
- [ ] Find a quiet place with no distractions
- [ ] Close other tabs and applications

### What You Need
- ✓ Exam link from your instructor
- ✓ Your full name
- ✓ Your valid email address
- ✓ Reliable internet connection
- ✓ 60-120 minutes (check duration with instructor)

### What You Can't Use
- ✗ External websites or search engines
- ✗ Books or notes (unless explicitly allowed)
- ✗ Other people's help (honest exam)
- ✗ Phones or other devices (unless specified)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Click the Exam Link
1. Instructor will send you a link like: `https://yoursite.com/exam/[code]`
2. Click the link or copy-paste it in browser
3. Press Enter

### Step 2: Enter Your Information
1. **Full Name**: Enter your complete name (exactly as on your record)
2. **Email**: Enter a valid email (e.g., firstname@domain.com)
   - Double-check for typos
   - No spaces before/after
   - Must have @ and domain

3. Click **Start Exam** button

### Step 3: Read the Instructions
- Read exam title and description
- Note the time limit (top right of screen)
- Note how many questions to answer
- Understand difficulty level

### Step 4: Answer Questions
- **For Multiple Choice**:
  - Click the radio button (●) next to your answer
  - Options are labeled A, B, C, D
  - Only one can be selected

- **For True/False**:
  - Click True or False
  - Only one can be selected

- **For Short Answer**:
  - Type your answer in the text box
  - Be clear and concise
  - No character limit usually

### Step 5: Navigate Questions
- **Next Button**: Go to next question
- **Previous Button**: Go to previous question
- **Question Counter**: Shows your position (e.g., "Question 3 of 50")
- **Progress Bar**: Shows percentage of answered questions

### Step 6: Check Your Progress
- Look at progress bar (top of screen)
- Shows % of questions answered
- See how many questions done vs total
- Monitor time remaining

### Step 7: Submit Your Exam
**IMPORTANT: Do NOT close the browser before submitting!**

When done:
1. Answer all questions you can
2. Click **Submit Exam** (appears on last question)
3. Wait for success message
4. DO NOT close browser during submission

The system will:
- Calculate your score
- Show if you passed or failed
- Display your percentage
- Show number correct/incorrect

### Step 8: View Results
- See your score percentage
- See if you passed
- Get feedback on performance
- Option to print results
- Returning to home

## During the Exam

### Timer
- Shown at top right in large numbers
- Counts down: 59m 45s → 59m 44s, etc.
- When timer hits 0:00, exam auto-submits
- **TIP**: Plan to finish 1-2 minutes early!

### Saving Answers
- Answers save automatically as you select them
- No need to click "Save" button
- Can review and change answers anytime
- Go back to any question with Previous button

### Making Changes
- Select a different answer anytime
- Just click a new option
- New answer replaces old one
- No penalty for changing answers

### Time Management Tips
- 1 minute per question is typical
- For 50 questions = ~50 minutes
- Spend more time on hard questions
- Skip very hard questions, come back
- Save 5 minutes for review before submitting

### Can't Answer a Question?
- Make your best guess
- Skip it and come back
- Don't leave questions blank unless optional
- Better to guess than leave blank

### If You Get Stuck
- Take a deep breath
- Read question again carefully
- Look for key words
- Eliminate obviously wrong answers
- Make educated guess
- Move on to next question

## After Submitting

### What Happens After "Submit"
1. System sends answers to server
2. Server calculates score
3. Shows results page
4. Results show in monitoring system

### Results Page Shows
- ✓ Your final score (percentage)
- ✓ Pass/Fail status (green=pass, red=fail)
- ✓ Number correct vs incorrect
- ✓ Your name and exam details
- ✓ Date and time taken
- ✓ Option to print results

### What's Next?
- **If Passed**: Congratulations! Certificate may be issued
- **If Failed**: Ask instructor about retake
- **Questions?**: Contact your instructor
- **Need Support?**: See troubleshooting guide

## Common Questions

### Q: Can I retake the exam?
**A**: Ask your instructor. Some exams allow retakes, others don't. Default is yes.

### Q: How do I see my score?
**A**: Results show immediately after submission on the results page.

### Q: Can the instructor see my answers?
**A**: Yes, instructors can view all attempts and answers in the admin monitoring dashboard.

### Q: What if I run out of time?
**A**: Exam auto-submits when timer reaches 0:00. Your current answers are submitted.

### Q: Can I pause the exam?
**A**: No, timer keeps running. Time is fixed from start to finish.

### Q: What if my internet disconnects?
**A**: Answers are saved locally. When internet returns, click Submit again.

### Q: Can I go back to previous questions?
**A**: Yes! Use Previous button to go back anytime.

### Q: Is there a penalty for wrong answers?
**A**: No. Only correct answers give points. Wrong answers get 0 points.

### Q: Can I use notes or books?
**A**: Only if instructor explicitly allowed it. Honest exam by default.

### Q: How is my score calculated?
**A**: (Questions Correct / Total Questions) × 100 = Score %

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

### Internet Disconnects
1. Reconnect to WiFi or mobile data
2. Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
3. Your answers should still be there
4. Try submitting again

### Browser Freezes
1. Wait 30 seconds
2. Force close browser (Alt+F4 or Cmd+Q)
3. Open new browser
4. Click exam link again
5. Your answers are saved, try submitting again

### Get an Error Message
1. Note the exact error message
2. Try the troubleshooting steps (see guide)
3. Try different browser
4. Contact instructor with error details
5. Have them check monitoring dashboard

### Can't Submit
1. Check if you answered at least 1 question
2. Check if email is valid format
3. Verify internet is working
4. Wait 1 minute, try again
5. Contact instructor if still fails

### See "Submission Failed" Message
1. System automatically retries 3 times
2. Wait for retries to complete
3. If still fails, contact instructor
4. Instructor can see status in monitoring

   - Your name
   - Exam name
   - What went wrong

## Score Interpretation

### What do the results mean?

**Passing Score** (usually 70%):
- Score ≥ 70% = **PASSED** ✓
  - You met the requirement
  - Certificate may be issued
  - Excellent performance

- Score < 70% = **FAILED** ✗
  - You didn't meet requirement
  - Ask about retake opportunity
  - Review material and try again

### Example Scores:
- 95% = Excellent
- 85% = Very Good
- 75% = Good
- 70% = Passed (passing score)
- 60% = Close but failed (retake recommended)
- 40% = Needs more study
